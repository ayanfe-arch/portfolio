About

Systems before screens.

I'm a Computer Science & Mathematics student at Obafemi Awolowo University, currently on an industrial placement at Web79 as a backend developer. I care about how data moves — how a request becomes a row in a database, how an order becomes a rider's next stop.

Most of what I build has no interface of its own. It's the layer other things stand on: authentication, schemas, endpoints that hold up under real use. I also teach frontend fundamentals — JavaScript, HTML, CSS — to students at my placement, which keeps me honest about explaining things clearly, not just making them work.
